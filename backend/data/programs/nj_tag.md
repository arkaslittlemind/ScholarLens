## Eligibility

- Must be a New Jersey resident for at least 12 consecutive months immediately prior to enrollment.
- Must be enrolled full-time in an approved undergraduate program at a New Jersey institution.
- Household income generally must fall below approximately $70,000-$80,000 for dependent students, with the exact threshold varying by family size (larger families qualify at higher incomes).
- Not eligible if a Bachelor's degree has already been earned (or an Associate's degree, if enrolled at a two-year college).
- Not eligible once the maximum number of allowable TAG payments has been received (9 payments for a Bachelor's degree, 5 for an Associate's degree).
- Not eligible if the student owes a refund on a federal or state grant, or is in default on a federal or state loan.
- Must maintain satisfactory academic progress.
- Must file the FAFSA or the NJ Alternative Financial Aid Application.

## Award

Up to $14,404 per academic year (2026-27).
