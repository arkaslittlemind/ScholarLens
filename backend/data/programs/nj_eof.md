## Eligibility

- Must demonstrate an educationally and economically disadvantaged background, as defined by the program's regulations.
- Must be a New Jersey resident for at least 12 consecutive months prior to the first semester of enrollment.
- Family income and assets must fall within the EOF Income Eligibility Scale for the applicable academic year, which varies by family size (published annually and updated for 2026-27).
- Must have graduated from high school or hold the equivalent of a high school diploma.
- Must apply and meet the additional academic admission criteria set by the specific participating institution.
- Must file the FAFSA or the NJ Alternative Financial Aid Application (NJAFAA).
- First-generation college student status is a factor considered under the program's regulatory definition, in addition to financial eligibility.

## Award

Grant funding plus academic support services (counseling, tutoring, developmental coursework) at participating New Jersey colleges and universities; exact grant amount varies by institution and financial need.
