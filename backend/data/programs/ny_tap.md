## Eligibility

- New York State net taxable income cannot exceed $125,000 for a dependent student.
- New York State net taxable income cannot exceed $60,000 for a married, independent student with no dependents.
- New York State net taxable income cannot exceed $30,000 for a single, independent student with no dependents.
- Must be a U.S. citizen or eligible noncitizen.
- Must be a New York State resident for at least 12 continuous months prior to enrollment.
- Must be enrolled full-time or part-time in an approved undergraduate program at a New York State college or university.
- Must have graduated from a U.S. high school, earned a GED / high school equivalency diploma, or passed a federally approved "Ability to Benefit" test.
- Must file the FAFSA and the TAP application (or the NYS DREAM Act application for students who do not meet standard citizenship/residency rules) each year.

## Award

Up to $5,665 per year, with the exact amount depending on income and other eligibility factors.
