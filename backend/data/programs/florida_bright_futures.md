## Eligibility

- Must complete 16 high school credits in the required distribution: 4 English, 4 Math, 3 Natural Science, 3 Social Studies, 2 World Languages.
- Must earn a weighted GPA of at least 3.50 on a 4.0 scale, calculated using only the required Bright Futures core courses.
- Must achieve a minimum SAT score of 1330 (Reading/Writing + Math only, no Essay) for the Class of 2026 and Class of 2027 (or the equivalent ACT score).
- Must complete at least 100 hours of community service, documented through the student's high school, by graduation.
- Must submit a complete Florida Financial Aid Application (FFAA) during senior year of high school, between October 1 and August 31 following graduation.
- Must be a Florida resident and U.S. citizen or eligible non-citizen.

## Award

100% of tuition and applicable fees at a Florida public university (a comparable dollar amount is awarded for Florida private universities).
