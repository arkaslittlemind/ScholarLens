## Eligibility

- Must be enrolled or accepted for enrollment as an undergraduate student.
- Must not have already earned a Bachelor's or first professional degree.
- Must file the FAFSA and demonstrate exceptional financial need (lower SAI than typical Pell-eligible students).
- Must be a U.S. citizen or eligible non-citizen.
- Must be enrolled at least half-time in an eligible program.
- Must maintain satisfactory academic progress as defined by the enrolling school.
- Must attend a school that participates in the FSEOG program (not all schools do).
- Priority is given to students with the lowest SAIs who are also receiving a Federal Pell Grant; remaining funds may go to eligible students with low SAIs who do not receive Pell.

## Award

Typically $100-$4,000 per year, depending on financial need, availability of funds at the institution, and other aid received. Unlike the Pell Grant, funding is limited per school and awarded on a first-come, first-served basis among eligible students.
