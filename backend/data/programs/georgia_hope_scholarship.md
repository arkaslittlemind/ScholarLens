## Eligibility

- Must graduate from a Georgia high school with a minimum 3.00 GPA (as calculated by the Georgia Student Finance Commission).
- Must earn a minimum of four full-rigor credits from the Academic Rigor Course List.
- Must be a Georgia resident.
- Must be a U.S. citizen or eligible non-citizen.
- Must be enrolled in a degree-seeking undergraduate program, pursuing a first Bachelor's degree (not available if a Bachelor's degree has already been earned).
- To remain eligible in college, must maintain a minimum 3.00 cumulative HOPE GPA, checked after the 30th, 60th, and 90th attempted semester hours, and at the end of each spring semester.

## Award

Pays a set amount per credit hour toward tuition at eligible Georgia public and private institutions (amount set annually by the state).
