## Eligibility (NJ STARS, initial award at a New Jersey county college)

- Must be a New Jersey resident and a recent New Jersey high school graduate.
- Must have an unweighted high school GPA of 3.0 or higher.
- Must rank in the top 15% of your graduating class, measured at the end of either junior or senior year.
- Must have completed 4 years of high school English and 3 years of high school math.
- Must enroll full-time at a New Jersey county college.
- To remain a NJ STARS recipient, must maintain a minimum cumulative GPA of 3.0 starting from the third semester of enrollment.

## Eligibility (NJ STARS II, transfer to a four-year institution)

- Must have completed an Associate's degree under NJ STARS.
- Must graduate with a GPA of 3.25 or higher.
- Household income must be less than $250,000.
- Must meet all other NJ STARS program requirements.

## Award

Full tuition at a New Jersey county college (NJ STARS); additional grant toward tuition at a four-year NJ institution after transfer (NJ STARS II).
